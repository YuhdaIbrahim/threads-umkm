Data Deletion Instructions

App: Threads API · Operator: Yuhda Ibrahim · Last updated: October 28, 2026

The "Threads API" app does not keep a separate database of your Threads data. This page explains how to stop the App from accessing your account and how to ask us to delete anything connected to it.

1. Remove the App's access to your Threads account

  1. Open Threads and go to Settings → Account → Website permissions.
  2. Find "Threads API" in the list of connected apps.
  3. Remove it. The access token issued to the App then stops working.

2. Ask us to delete your data

To request deletion of any information connected to your account, such as stored credentials or execution history that includes your Threads user ID or username, send an email to testingneo536@gmail.com with:

  • The subject line "Data deletion request".
  • Your Threads username.

We will confirm by email and delete the information within 30 days of verifying the request.

3. What is deleted

Deletion covers your stored access token, and any execution history held by the workflow tool that includes your user ID or username. Public posts remain on Threads, because they belong to Threads and their authors, and we have no control over them.

See also our Privacy Policy.
