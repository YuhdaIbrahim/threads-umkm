Privacy Policy

App: Threads API · Operator: Yuhda Ibrahim · Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the "Threads API" app (the "App") accesses and how it is used. The App is a small automation tool that connects to the Threads API provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. to search public posts by keyword, for example to help small businesses (UMKM) in Indonesia understand what people are discussing about topics such as food.

1. Information we access

When you authorize the App with your Threads account, the App may access:

  • Your Threads user ID and username, and a Threads access token issued by Meta for your account.
  • The search keywords that are entered into the App.
  • Public Threads post information returned by the Threads API for those keywords, such as post text, username, link, timestamp, and like, reply, repost and quote counts.

The App only requests the Threads permissions it needs to operate (such as threads_basic and threads_keyword_search).

2. How we use the information

Information is used only to run the keyword search and show the results to the person operating the App. We do not use it for advertising, we do not build profiles of individual people, and we do not sell, rent or share it with third parties.

3. Storage and retention

The App does not keep search results in a separate database or file. Results are processed when a search runs. The workflow tool that runs the App (n8n) may keep a temporary execution history on the server where it is installed; this history is cleared periodically and can be deleted at any time on request. Your access token is stored only in the credential store of that workflow tool and is not shared with anyone.

4. Sharing of information

We do not share Threads data with third parties. Data is exchanged only between the App and Meta's Threads API, over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. We may disclose information if required to do so by law.

5. Security

Access tokens and credentials are kept in a protected credential store and are not published. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and data deletion

You can stop the App from accessing your Threads account at any time by removing it from your Threads account settings (Settings → Account → Website permissions). You can also ask us to delete any data connected to your account by following the steps on our Data Deletion Instructions page.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.
